# Cross-sectorial integrated digital services Enabling energy Localized INnovation and community Empowerment

**CELINE** is a Horizon Europe research and innovation project building a harmonised digital framework for renewable energy communities (RECs). It enables citizens, businesses, and local authorities to co-manage local energy resources — from production and consumption to peer-to-peer trading and community governance.

[celineproject.eu](https://www.celineproject.eu)

---

## What CELINE builds

Energy communities are reshaping the energy landscape, but they need tools. CELINE delivers:

| Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| **Digital platform** | Secure, interoperable systems for energy data collection, management, and exchange |
| **Local energy exchange** | Infrastructure for peer-to-peer energy trading and new community business models |
| **Citizen participation** | Co-design methodologies, participatory governance, and training programmes |
| **Policy frameworks** | Standardised guidelines aligned with EU energy community regulations |

## Demonstration sites

CELINE is validated in three real energy communities across Europe:

- **Spain** — Valencia, coordinated by SAPIENS and CIMNE
- **Italy** — managed by Green Land and Fondazione Bruno Kessler (FBK)
- **Finland** — City of Lappeenranta, with LUT University
